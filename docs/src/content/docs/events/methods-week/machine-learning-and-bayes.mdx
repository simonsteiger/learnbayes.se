[Martin Trapp](https://trappmartin.github.io/website/) is a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science at Aalto University, Finland.
His research focuses on probabilistic machine learning, uncertainty quantification, and reliable and trustworthy AI.

His talk is titled **The synergies and challenges at the intersection of machine learning and Bayesian statistics**.
The talk will touch on the following topics:

- Quick primer on deep learning
- A brief overview of Bayesian deep learning:  promises, challenges, and the reality
- A brief overview on deep learning for Bayesian inference with focus on amortisation techniques
