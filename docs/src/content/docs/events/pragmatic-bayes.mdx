Matrin Modrák is a lecturer and statistician at the Second Faculty of Medicine at Charles University in Prague.

In his seminar, Martin will talk about a pragmatist approach to Bayesian inference, which implies using Bayesian tools without fully embracing Bayesian epistemology, namely the subjective approach to probability that is typically at the core of Bayesian philosophy.
