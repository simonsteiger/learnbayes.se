**Welcome to the Methods Week!**
Monday through Thursday (12–15 May) is packed with fantastic seminars and workshops in this all-you-can-eat Bayesian buffet (or smörgåsbord), topped off with a **[Networking Event](/information/networking-event)**, promising exciting keynote speakers and a panel discussion.
Feel free to mix and match among the events as you see fit and your schedule allows.

**You do not have to have attended any previous events in the seminar series and no sign up is required.**
